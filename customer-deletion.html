Delete Your AWFER Account

Request deletion of your AWFER Customer account and associated personal data.

Delete your account from the app

If you have access to the AWFER Customer application, you can delete your account directly from your account settings.

  1. Open the AWFER Customer application.
  2. Sign in to your AWFER account.
  3. Open your Profile.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Review the account deletion information.
  6. Confirm that you want to delete your account.

After you confirm the deletion, AWFER will process the request and delete your account and associated personal data in accordance with our data retention requirements.

Cannot access the app?

If you cannot sign in to the AWFER Customer application or otherwise cannot use the in-app account deletion feature, you can request account deletion by contacting AWFER.

Please contact us using the email address below and clearly state that you want your AWFER Customer account deleted.

Request Account Deletion

What happens when you delete your account?

When an account deletion request is confirmed, AWFER will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymise personal information associated with your customer account, subject to information that we are required or permitted to retain under applicable law.

Account-related information that is no longer required will be removed from active systems in accordance with AWFER's deletion and data retention procedures.

Important: Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required for legal, regulatory, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, dispute-resolution, or other lawful purposes.

Information that may be deleted

Depending on the information associated with your account, account deletion may include deletion or anonymisation of information such as:

  • Customer account information;
  • Contact information associated with your account;
  • Authentication and account profile information;
  • Saved account preferences;
  • Information associated with your customer account;
  • Other personal information that is no longer required for a lawful purpose.

Certain transaction, trip, security, or legal records may be retained where required by applicable law or where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Account deletion requests

For security purposes, AWFER may need to verify that an account deletion request has been made by the account holder before processing the request.

If you submit a deletion request by email, we may request reasonable information necessary to verify ownership of the account. We will not request your password or other authentication credentials by email.

Once the request has been verified, AWFER will process the deletion in accordance with applicable laws and our data retention requirements.
