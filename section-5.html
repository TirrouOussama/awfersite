SECTION 05 — THE FUTURE
The shift has already started.
Most just haven’t seen it yet.
Centralized control is not scaling.

It is collapsing under its own weight.
01 — The End of Extraction
Systems that depend on extracting value from their own network cannot sustain themselves indefinitely.

Pressure accumulates — until the system breaks.
02 — A New Standard
Infrastructure that aligns incentives will replace platforms that distort them.

Not because it is idealistic — but because it is more efficient.
03 — Network Ownership
The future is not controlled by a single entity.

It is operated by the participants themselves — coordinated through infrastructure.
04 — Inevitable Transition
This shift does not require belief.

It follows from how systems behave under pressure.
The only question is not if.
It is who builds it first.
